Privacy policy

FE Prep Planner · last updated October 21, 2026

What the site stores

  • Your email address and a password, handled by Google Firebase Authentication. The site never sees your password.
  • Your plan: exam date, the hours you said you can study, your study sessions and which ones you checked off.
  • Busy time ranges from your calendar (start and end times only), so the plan can be rebuilt around them. No event titles, descriptions, attendees or locations.

This data lives in Google Cloud Firestore under your account. Security rules let only you read or change it.

Calendar access

  • Google Calendar: the site asks for two permissions. calendar.freebusy shows when you are busy, not what the events are. calendar.app.created lets the site create and manage one calendar called “FE Study Plan”. It cannot see or change your other calendars.
  • Outlook: the site reads the start, end and free/busy status of your events and creates one calendar called “FE Study Plan”.
  • .ics upload: the file is read in your browser. Only busy start and end times are kept.

Access tokens stay in your browser for the session and are never stored on a server. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. There are no analytics or tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and plan, email alexander.guzmanme02@gmail.com. To remove calendar access, delete the “FE Study Plan” calendar and revoke the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or account.live.com/consent/Manage (Microsoft).
